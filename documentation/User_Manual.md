# User Manual

## 1. How to Open Website

Start the backend and frontend, then open:

```text
http://localhost:5173
```

## 2. How to Register

1. Click **Register** in the navbar.
2. Enter name, email, password, phone, city, and address.
3. Click **Register**.
4. The system logs you in after successful registration.

## 3. How to Login

1. Click **Login**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Login**.

## 4. How to Browse Medicines

1. Open the home page or click **Products**.
2. View medicine cards with image, name, category, price, and stock.

## 5. How to Search Medicine

1. Use the search bar on home page or products page.
2. Type medicine name or keyword.
3. Click **Search**.
4. Matching medicines will appear.

## 6. How to View Categories

1. Open the navbar category dropdown or home category section.
2. Click a category such as Fever, Cough, Vitamins, or Dehydration.
3. Products from that category will appear.

## 7. How to View Medicine Details

1. Click **Details** on a product card.
2. The product detail page shows dosage, pack size, manufacturer, stock, batch number, expiry date, price, and prescription requirement.

## 8. How to Add Medicine to Cart

1. Login first.
2. Click **Add** on product card or **Add to Cart** on product details page.
3. The cart count in navbar will update.

## 9. How to Update Cart

1. Click **Cart** in navbar.
2. Change quantity using the quantity input.
3. The cart total updates automatically.
4. Click **Remove** to delete an item.

## 10. How to Checkout

1. Open cart and click **Proceed to Checkout**.
2. Fill full name, phone, city, postal code, and address.
3. Select payment method.
4. Add notes if needed.
5. Click **Place Order**.

## 11. How to Upload Prescription if Required

If any medicine in your cart requires prescription:

1. Checkout page will show a warning.
2. Click prescription image upload input.
3. Select jpg, jpeg, png, or webp image.
4. Place order.

## 12. How to View Order History

1. Login.
2. Click your name in the navbar.
3. Click **My Orders**.
4. View all your previous orders.

## 13. How to View Order Details

1. Open **My Orders**.
2. Click **View** on an order.
3. View items, status, shipping details, payment status, and invoice link.

## 14. How to Update Profile

1. Click your name in the navbar.
2. Click **Profile**.
3. Edit name, email, phone, city, or address.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

## 15. How to Contact Pharmacy

1. Click **Contact**.
2. Enter name, email, subject, and message.
3. Click **Send Message**.

## 16. How to Logout

1. Click your name in the navbar.
2. Click **Logout**.

## Important Medicine Warning

Pill Stream does not provide medical advice. Some medicines may require a valid prescription. Please consult a doctor or pharmacist before using any medicine.
